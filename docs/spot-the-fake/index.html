A little game about AI skills

Which bar is
the fake?

One task. Three score ranges. Can you spot the same-length fake? Pick a bar, reveal the labels, and read the receipt.

An AI answered with a skill, with unrelated text of the same length, and with no skill text. The labels are hidden. Tap the bar you think is the fake.

The game needs JavaScript. Read the tasks and their readings in Report 017 and Report 018.

A guess is not a verdict.

This game moves no verdict and says nothing about a skill beyond these readings. Your tally stays on this screen. It is never stored or sent.

Each bar is the average plus or minus one spread across repeated answers, clipped to the score scale. The dot is the average. A wider bar means more variation, not more certainty.

These are existing receipts, with no new model run. The rounds are a fixed shuffle, chosen without looking at scores. Reports 017 and 018 keep the full context.

Words with a dotted line link to the glossary, where each is explained in plain words.
