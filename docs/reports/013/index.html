Clearly higher (or lower): in that run the spread of scores for the two models did not overlap, and their averages were at least 0.05 apart. A model’s spread is its average plus or minus the usual variation of its answers’ scores.

No clear difference: there were enough answers to see a gap of 0.05, and the spreads overlapped or the gap was smaller.

Too few answers to tell: the scores were too spread out, or too few, to see a gap of 0.05 either way. It does not mean the models scored the same.
