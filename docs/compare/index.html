How it scoresPass/fail graders (regex, tool_used, tool_order, file_exists, llm judge, baseline judge); a run scores the (optionally weighted) fraction of graders passed; a case scores the mean across runs and passes at --threshold (default 1.0). An llm grader passes on at least two of three judge votes (source, read 30 September 2026)Per-case assertions graded by rule_based, script or agent_judge (LLM rubric, pass_threshold default 0.7); a case is PASS when all assertions pass; results roll up to pass_rate (source, read 30 September 2026; source, read 30 September 2026; source, read 30 September 2026)Five dimension scores from 0.0 to 1.0 with fixed bands (PASS at 0.50 or above, NEUTRAL 0.40 to below 0.50, FAIL below 0.40); an agent passes only when every dimension passes. Skill Lift (with-skill minus baseline) has its own bands (PASS at +0.05 or above, FAIL at -0.10 or below) and is diagnostic, not the verdict (source, read 30 September 2026; source, read 30 September 2026; source, read 2 October 2026; source, read 2 October 2026)A judge model grades each output pass/fail against the eval's assertions (expected_output is promoted to an assertion when none are given); deterministic tool_assertions are also available; results roll up to pass_rate (source, read 30 September 2026; source, read 30 September 2026)An LLM judge scores each response against rubrics anchored at 0.80; the lift is the with-skill mean minus the baseline mean; the verdict is PASSED, NO_EFFECT, REGRESSED or UNDERPOWERED against a 0.05 effect floor, and a case is called improved or regressed only when the bands do not overlap and the move clears the floor (source, read 2 October 2026; source, read 2 October 2026; source, read 2 October 2026; source, read 30 September 2026; source, read 2 October 2026)
